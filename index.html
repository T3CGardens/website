01 / THE PLACE

T3C Gardens is a family-owned pub, grill, accommodation and leisure destination in Lilayi. Wander beside the ponds, spend time in the gardens, or find a corner to share a meal.

Get to know the gardens ↘

Find your own pace.

Each garden has its own feel. Come for a regular day out, or get in touch if you are planning something special.

01 / LILAYI 1

Pondside days

Floating Bar
& Homely Home

At Lilayi 1, the Floating Bar sits over a large man-made pond, with fishing on both sides and a floating platform inspired by a pontoon. Homely Home is a four-bedroom former family residence with two living rooms, a dining room and kitchen; ask the team about its current rental arrangements. A converted garage bar sits by another pond, near garden seating, a playground, swings, a trampoline and a swimming pool. One-bedroom apartments are also part of the offering; contact the team about current availability.

03 / MAIN EVENTS GARDEN

Made for moments

Main Events Garden

A garden bar, four connected ponds and landscaped grounds create a setting for weddings, birthdays and other gatherings. Fishing is an important attraction. You can also visit the grounds without hiring the venue for a private event.
